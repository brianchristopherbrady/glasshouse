# 02 — Ontology: Foam, Float, Time

## The Foam [HARD CANON]

The underlying relational substrate, or relational potential, of reality.

**Do not describe the Foam as:** a database, a graph database, a simulation, a computer, an information ledger, or any other computational metaphor treated as literally true. Technical characters (B, Noor, Asterion documentation) may *reach for* computational metaphors because those are the tools available to them — this is characterization, not ontology, and the metaphors are always incomplete. See `meta/canon` §1 for the same instruction stated as a guardrail.

Relationships are not metadata attached to otherwise-complete objects. **Relationships participate in making things what they are.** A person is not an isolated entity with properties attached; a person exists through a dense, overlapping web of relations — to other people, their own body, memory, language, places, objects, habits, institutions, obligations, love, injury, history, expectation, identity, the stories others tell about them, and themselves.

Do not literalize this as a clean node-edge graph. Human relationships are braids — overlapping compositions, pressures, and affinities that cannot always be cleanly separated. Full braid mechanics: `meta_correction.md` §Relationships as Braids.

The Foam does not think, judge, or intend in any sense that has been established. It responds. Whether it has *anything* resembling preference or intention beneath that response is deliberately unresolved — see `16_open_questions.md`.

## The Float [HARD CANON]

The presently stabilized, collectively inhabited configuration of reality.

Consensus is not voting. It is not what most people consciously agree to after discussion. It is the enormous, largely unconscious accumulation of:

- recognition
- expectation
- interpretation
- conscious participation
- material persistence
- causal consequence
- language
- social agreement
- habit
- institutional reinforcement

The Float is what becomes inhabitable enough to be experienced as ordinary reality. It is not synonymous with truth. It is what remains mutually usable. Someone encountering a chair does not vote for its chairness; they simply participate in the relationship *that is a chair*. The same continuous, unconscious participation stabilizes *that is my wife*, *that man works here*, *this happened yesterday*, *this is my name*.

**Power is not distributed equally within the Float** [WORKING CANON, carried forward from `meta/themes` §Power and the Float — this is compatible with and extends `meta/canon`, which does not currently foreground it]. Institutions, governments, corporations, and credentialed authorities can press harder, longer, and more deliberately than individuals can. What stabilizes into the Float reflects who had the resources to press most consistently — not a conspiracy, because it requires no conspiracy. This is why the Choir experiences itself as maintenance rather than control: it is protecting what already exists, which is also protecting what those with power built. See `meta_institutions.md` for the full institutional treatment.

## Physics in the Float [HARD CANON]

Conventional physical laws remain overwhelmingly reliable. People can build bridges. Engineers can calculate loads. Objects fall downward. This predictability is not a limit imposed from outside the Float. It is a consequence of physics being among the deepest and most reinforced relational structures in consensus reality — supported by immense accumulated agreement, material persistence, causal consequence, and institutional habit.

"Deeply stable" is not the same as metaphysically absolute.

Under sufficient relational contradiction, semantic pressure, or correction-produced instability, the Float can sustain behavior inconsistent with ordinary physical expectation. This should remain rare enough to matter and common enough that Devs have professional categories for it. Most people were born into this world. They do not spend their days astonished. They complain. They make videos. They buy continuity insurance. They argue about whether an anomaly is real. They continue going to work.

An ordinary chair throwing itself downstairs is alarming. It is not proof that the universe has collapsed. There are professionals for that.

**Do not make Meridian feel like a dream where anything can happen at any moment.** Reality is stable. It is simply not absolute.

## Time [HARD CANON — this is a new, explicit foreclosure, not previously stated this plainly anywhere in `meta/`]

Bloomrot is not a time-travel story. There are no branching timelines. Asterion does not reach backward and physically rewrite a past moment. Avoid unnecessary metaphysical commitment about whether past moments literally continue to exist somewhere — that question should stay philosophically slippery rather than becoming solved physics.

Operationally: everything that matters to the Float exists *now*. The present contains relationships to what humans call the past through memory, records, bodies, scars, architecture, contracts, photographs, documents, habits, institutions, objects, language, ritual, identity, and consequences. Correction alters the currently recognized relational structure of the Float — it does not "change Tuesday." It changes what relationships are currently real and recognized.

**Working distinction:** correction does not alter a historical timeline; it alters the present relational reality through which history exists now.

### What Is Known [ontology — established]

- The Float can be altered deliberately through Chrysanthemum-mediated correction.
- Correction acts on present relational structure, not on a discrete past event.
- The Foam retains displaced relational pressure after a relationship is severed from the Float (see `meta_bloomrot.md`).

### What Institutional Science Believes [doctrine — may be wrong; see README's central distinction]

- Asterion's Continuity doctrine holds that successful correction severs recognized provenance completely and that what remains "is expected to decay" (quoted directly from Mote's translation of Continuity doctrine, drafted in chapter_1). This is the doctrine Noor's discovery falsifies.
- The Choir's working model treats semantics as an *input* to a controlled process (consciousness → intention → Chrysanthemum → Foam), not as a process capable of running without an author. See `meta_bloomrot.md` §The Critical Noor Discovery.

### What Remains Unknown [OPEN QUESTION — see `16_open_questions.md` for the master list]

- Whether past moments have any continued existence independent of present relational structure.
- Whether the Foam's non-choosing behavior conceals something closer to intention at a scale or timescale nobody has observed.
