# Council Minutes

Kept by the Mayor. Newest entries at the bottom.

## Day 1 of the Founding

- The city is founded. The charter is ratified by three people and a goose.
- Old Town is declared the first district.
- Outcome: APPROVED (P-001), cost ₫1,500.

— the Mayor

## Day 2 of the Founding

- Harbor Row is founded so the city has somewhere to keep its boats and its feelings.
- Outcome: APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS (P-002), cost ₫1,500.

— the Mayor

## Day 3 of the Founding

- Emergency session: the goose was hungry. Bread was procured. Order was restored.
- A citizen proposed a bouncy castle on Wobble Hill. The council laughed, then filed it properly.
- Outcome: bouncy castle DENIED (P-003). Bread cost ₫12. One lost tourist paid ₫2,500 in tourist tax.

— the Mayor
