# Old Town

- **Code:** OT
- **Squares:** B3, C3
- **Zoning:** historic-whimsical
- **Founded:** Day 1 of the Founding
- **Population:** 1,204 humans, 1 goose (protected, see Charter Article V)

## Vibe

Cobblestones that are slightly too round, lamp posts that lean in to listen, and a bakery that has been "closing in five minutes" since Day 1.

## Landmarks

- **The Clock Tower** — 9 floors (11 counting the vibes). Chimes whenever anyone says "actually".
- **The Bench of Reasonable Comfort** — 1 floor, if a bench can have floors. The city's first legally required place to sit down.

## Inspection notes

- Day 1: APPROVED. Clock Tower measured at exactly the height limit, which is suspicious but legal. The goose abstained. — the Building Inspector
