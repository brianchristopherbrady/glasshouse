# Harbor Row

- **Code:** HR
- **Squares:** B2
- **Zoning:** nautical
- **Founded:** Day 2 of the Founding
- **Population:** 318 humans, 40 boats, 1 octopus with a library card

## Vibe

Salt air, creaking piers, and fishmongers who insist every fish is "the one that got away, but came back".

## Landmarks

- **The Lighthouse That Winks** — 6 floors. Blinks in Morse code; mostly it says "hello".
- **The Pier of Contemplation** — 1 floor. Long enough to sit on and think about boats.

## Inspection notes

- Day 2: APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS. The lighthouse must stop winking at ships. It has not stopped. — the Building Inspector
