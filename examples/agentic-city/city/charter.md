# The Charter of Agentic City

*Ratified on Day 1 of the Founding by a unanimous vote of everyone who showed up (three people and a goose).*

## Article I — The City

Agentic City is a city made entirely of words. If it is not written down in `city/`, it does not exist. If it is written down, it is binding, no matter how unreasonable.

## Article II — The Offices

1. **The Mayor** sets the agenda, records every decision in the council minutes, and delegates the work. The Mayor may not draw on the map.
2. **The City Planner** designs districts and landmarks and is the only office allowed to edit the map.
3. **The Building Inspector** reviews every new district and landmark against this charter and the zoning code, and records a verdict in the permit register.
4. **The Treasurer** records every expense and income in the ledger. The treasury may never go below ₫0. (It has come close. The goose was involved.)
5. **The Town Crier** reports on the city's affairs in the Gazette. The Crier may exaggerate the tone but may not invent facts that contradict the records.

## Article III — Building Rules

1. No structure may be taller than the Clock Tower, which is 9 floors (11 if you count the ones that are just vibes).
2. Every district must contain at least one place to sit down.
3. Bridges must be named after something that does not deserve a bridge.
4. Nothing may be built on Wobble Hill. Wobble Hill knows what it did.

## Article IV — Money

All prices are in civic doubloons (₫). The Treasurer keeps the official price list in the civic budget rules.

## Article V — The Goose

The Goose of Old Town is a protected citizen with full voting rights. The Goose's decisions are final and may not be appealed.
