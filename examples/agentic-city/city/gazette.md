# The Agentic Gazette

*"All the news that fits, and some that doesn't." Edited by the Town Crier. Newest edition first.*

## Edition 3 — Day 3 of the Founding

### GOOSE HUNGER CRISIS AVERTED; WOBBLE HILL REMAINS UNBOUNCED

Emergency bread arrived at 4 o'clock sharp and the goose has issued no statement, which experts interpret as approval. Separately, the Building Inspector denied a bouncy castle on Wobble Hill, citing Article III.4 and "a feeling".

> **Goose Watch:** Full, smug, and eyeing the bakery.

## Edition 2 — Day 2 of the Founding

### HARBOR ROW OPENS; LIGHTHOUSE ACCUSED OF FLIRTING

The city's second district opened to the sound of creaking piers. Approval came with one condition: the lighthouse must stop winking at ships. Sources close to the lighthouse say it "can't help it".

> **Goose Watch:** Suspicious of boats.

## Edition 1 — Day 1 of the Founding

### CITY FOUNDED, ALMOST ENTIRELY ON PURPOSE

Three people and a goose ratified the charter this morning. Old Town was declared the first district and the Clock Tower began chiming immediately.

> **Goose Watch:** Honked twice. Interpreted as a yes.
